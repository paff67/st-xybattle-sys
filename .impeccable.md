## Design Context

### Users
The primary user is the author and operator of a SillyTavern role-play world. They use the battle workbench while writing a scene to inspect AI-adjudicated combat facts, choose a protagonist action, and keep the narrative model aligned with committed state.

### Brand Personality
Tactile, cinematic, disciplined. The interface should feel like a focused side-view duel instrument: immediate enough for play, precise enough for auditing, and quiet enough to keep attention on the scene.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark indigo stage lighting with warm parchment-gold action accents and restrained water-blue highlights. The main workbench is a classic horizontal fighting-game tableau with two readable silhouettes, radial technique petals, status ribbons, and a grounded action dock. Settings, imports, and developer logs remain utility surfaces outside the stage.

### Design Principles
- Put the current duel and next action above implementation detail.
- Keep left/right spatial roles stable: protagonist left, opponent right, arena state between them.
- Reveal full technique definitions progressively from a compact petal; never replace source text with opaque abbreviations.
- Distinguish observed, inferred, and unknown enemy information before it can become an action affordance.
- Preserve keyboard access, reduced motion, and a usable narrow-screen fallback.
